Inside the Four Pillars: How Memora, Evrocks, Coretez, and Linkers Work Together

By ikhsanadzani November 6, 2026 · 08:45 WIB (UTC+07:00) · 3 min read

JAKARTA, Nov 6 (Radova) — Most cultural heritage and academic institutions run separate, disconnected stacks for their library catalog, archival fonds, digital media assets, and scholarly repository. Radova Linux unifies all four workflows on a single operating-system foundation.

Role of each pillar

  • Memora (Library Management): Handles bibliographic cataloging, circulation desks, patron records, and OPAC discovery.
  • Evrocks (Archive Management): Structures multi-level archival descriptions, provenance trees, and retention schedules.
  • Coretez (Digital Asset Management): Indexes high-resolution scans, audio/video masters, EXIF/IPTC metadata, and derivative previews.
  • Linkers (Institutional Repository): Publishes academic papers, theses, and research datasets with persistent identifiers and citation metadata.

Shared operating-system conventions

Because all four applications ship together inside Radova Linux, they share a consistent filesystem layout under /var/lib/radova/, unified backup snapshot hooks, and pre-tuned local database services—eliminating the fragile glue scripts typically required to maintain four separate web platforms.
